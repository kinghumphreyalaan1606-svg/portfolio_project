Building worlds in text before I build them in code.

I'm an IT student who spends as much time inventing fictional worlds as debugging real ones. Between assignments, I write fanfiction, draw, and lose track of time in games I probably should have finished by now.

Learn More

I write fanfiction, mostly because I enjoy living in worlds after the credits roll and asking what else could happen there. That habit spilled over into drawing, and eventually into just liking games in general — not only playing them, but noticing how they're built.

What actually pulls me in, though, is talking about how those worlds work underneath the story: worldbuilding logic, economics, politics — the systems that decide why a kingdom rises or a character makes the choice they make. I bring that same instinct to code: I like knowing why something works, not just that it does.
